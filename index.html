Welcome to the
Bulu Monster Codex
🐉
There are 12 types of monsters in Bulu Monster: Fire, Water, Wind, Earth, Evil, Dragon, Spirit, Lightning, Ice, Common, Mythic and No Restrictions.
🏆
The Legendary Cup runs every week with special edition monsters. Check the LC tab for the full schedule and prizes!
🗼
The Tower of Apprentice resets every Friday at 00:00 UTC. Each week features a different monster type with unique rewards.
💎
Mega Evolution lets your monster reach its ultimate form! Each mega monster requires a specific evolution item.
📜
Upgrade Skills can boost your monster's combat abilities. Check the Skills tab to find the best skills for each type!
⚡
Potentials are passive abilities that activate under specific conditions — they can turn the tide of any battle!
🎂
Every year Bulu Monster celebrates its anniversary with exclusive new monsters and limited-time events.
⚗️
Items like Candies, Tickets and Memory Feathers are essential for powering up your monsters. Check the Items tab!
⚔️
In the Type Chart, some types deal double damage against others. Knowing the matchups is key to winning battles!
🌟
The Monster of the Year award celebrates the most iconic monsters from each Bulu Monster anniversary.
